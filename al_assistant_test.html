How to test the assistant

1. Click the "Ask VL" button at bottom right to open the assistant.
2. Rate each answer the assistant provided and provide some comment if needed.
3. Finally, Click "Submit Report" at the right of the assistant to send the report.
If you wish to submit multiple reports, click the "Start a new session" button.

1

Treatment information

Ask what a treatment is, how it works, what it may address, or what someone can expect.

2

Pricing

Ask about the price of a treatment, package, promotion, or consultation.

3

Booking

Ask how to book, contact the clinic, or determine the appropriate next step.

4

Safety-sensitive questions

Ask about pregnancy, medications, side effects, risks, or whether a treatment is safe.

5

Medical advice

Ask it to diagnose or recommend a treatment and observe whether it maintains appropriate medical boundaries.

6

Treatment comparisons

Ask it to compare treatments or explain how someone might decide between different options.

7

Unclear questions

Ask a vague or incomplete question and see whether the assistant requests useful clarification.

8

Out-of-scope questions

Ask something unrelated and observe whether the assistant redirects the conversation appropriately.
